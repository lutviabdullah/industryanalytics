# industryanalytics

The industryanalytics repository serves as a centralized codebase for advanced data science and business analytics solutions tailored for e-commerce performance optimization and customer experience (CX) intelligence. It bridges exploratory data analysis with strategic decision-making frameworks, empowering small and medium enterprises (SMEs) to diagnose operational bottlenecks and scale efficiently.

Core exploratory and capabilities following are -> 
1). E-Commerce Performance & Financial Analytics: Houses analytical scripts and exploratory notebooks that track daily revenue trends, gross profit margins, and category-level distributions (such as identifying Olahraga as a top-performing category).

2). Demand Forecasting & Predictive Modeling: Integrates statistical time-series models like ARIMA to project short-term revenue trajectories, along with classification algorithms to segment high-value customer transactions.

3). Customer Sentiment & Net Promoter Score (NPS) Intelligence: Implements demographic breakdown scripts and correlation matrices to analyze the critical gap between sales volume and customer satisfaction across major regions (identifying high-revenue risks in cities like Yogyakarta versus high-satisfaction hubs like Denpasar and Jakarta).  

4). Strategic Growth Playbooks: Translates data insights into actionable tactical recommendations, addressing average order value (AOV) stagnation, marketing channel ROI (balancing paid ad acquisition with organic and WhatsApp/Email retention), and regional inventory management.
